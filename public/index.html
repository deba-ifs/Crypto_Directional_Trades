⚙️ System Execution Architecture & Pipeline
Step 1: REST Ingestion & IST Timezone Normalization
Queries Delta Exchange India REST endpoints every 30 seconds for live OHLCV candles (`/v2/history/candles`). Epoch UTC timestamps are converted to IST (UTC + 5:30).
Step 2: Vectorized Analytics & Indicator Matrix
Computes technical indicators using native `pandas` and `numpy`: EMA (20, 50, 200), ATR (14), Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std), Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR), TTM Squeeze Compression Duration, ADX (14), and Volume SMA (20).
Step 3: Signal Classification & Directional Filtering
Evaluates breakout rules: Squeeze release trigger, Volume surge ($\ge 1.15 \times \text{SMA}_{20}$), Trend alignment ($\text{Price} > \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Long, $\text{Price} < \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Short), and Momentum positivity ($\text{Squeeze\_Mom} > 0$).
Step 4: Fixed-Target Position Locking Engine
Upon trade trigger, entry price, stop-loss, and take-profit targets are locked in state memory. Levels remain frozen across API refreshes until intra-candle price action hits TP or SL.

🧮 Mathematical Formulas & Derivations

1. TTM Volatility Squeeze Condition

$$\text{BB}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \mu_{20} \pm 2.0 \cdot \sigma_{20}, \quad \text{KC}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \text{EMA}_{20} \pm 1.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_On} = (\text{BB}_{\text{Lower}} > \text{KC}_{\text{Lower}}) \land (\text{BB}_{\text{Upper}} < \text{KC}_{\text{Upper}})$$

2. Fixed Entry, Take-Profit Target, and Stop-Loss Levels

$$\text{Target}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(2.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(1.0 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$ $$\text{Target}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(2.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(1.0 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$
