⚙️ System Execution Architecture & Pipeline
Step 1: REST Ingestion & Multi-Timeframe Parsing
Queries Delta Exchange India REST endpoints every 30 seconds for live OHLCV candles (`/v2/history/candles`) and active options chain metrics (`/v2/tickers`). Data is routed dynamically based on the selected timeframe toggle (15-Minute or 1-Hour).
Step 2: Vectorized Analytics & Indicator Matrix
Computes technical indicators using native `pandas` and `numpy`: EMA (20, 50, 200), ATR (14), Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std), Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR), TTM Squeeze Compression Duration, ADX (14), and Volume SMA (20).
Step 3: Signal Classification & Directional Filtering
Evaluates strict breakout rules: Squeeze release trigger, Volume surge ($\ge 1.15 \times \text{SMA}_{20}$), Trend alignment ($\text{Price} > \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Long, $\text{Price} < \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Short), and Momentum positivity ($\text{Squeeze\_Mom} > 0$).
Step 4: Fixed-Target Position Locking & State Machine
Upon trade trigger, entry price, stop-loss, and take-profit targets are locked in state memory. Levels remain frozen across API refreshes until intra-candle price action hits TP or SL.
Step 5: Adaptive Self-Enhancement Feedback Loop
When an active trade closes, its outcome ($\text{TARGET\_HIT}$ vs $\text{SL\_HIT}$) is logged in the Signal Journal. The adaptive engine automatically tunes the minimum ADX threshold and ATR multipliers based on rolling win rate and profit factor.

🧮 Mathematical Formulas & Quantitative Derivations

1. TTM Volatility Squeeze Condition

Detects energy buildup when Bollinger Bands compress inside Keltner Channels.

$$\text{BB}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \mu_{20} \pm 2.0 \cdot \sigma_{20}$$ $$\text{KC}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \text{EMA}_{20} \pm 1.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_On} = (\text{BB}_{\text{Lower}} > \text{KC}_{\text{Lower}}) \land (\text{BB}_{\text{Upper}} < \text{KC}_{\text{Upper}})$$

2. Average True Range ($\text{ATR}_{14}$)

Measures market volatility to scale stop-loss and take-profit targets dynamically.

$$\text{TR}_t = \max\left(H_t - L_t, \, \vert{}H_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}, \, \vert{}L_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}\right)$$ $$\text{ATR}_{14} = \frac{1}{14} \sum_{i=0}^{13} \text{TR}_{t-i}$$

3. TTM Momentum Histogram

Calculates linear regression delta against the midpoint of price extremes and EMA 20.

$$\Delta_{\text{Mid}} = \frac{\max(H_{20}) + \min(L_{20})}{2}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_Mom} = C_t - \left( \frac{\Delta_{\text{Mid}} + \text{EMA}_{20}}{2} \right)$$

4. Average Directional Index ($\text{ADX}_{14}$)

Filters out non-trending market chop by requiring trend intensity to breach adaptive threshold $\theta_{\text{ADX}}$.

$$+\text{DM} = H_t - H_{t-1}, \quad -\text{DM} = L_{t-1} - L_t$$ $$+\text{DI}_{14} = 100 \cdot \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(+\text{DM})}{\text{ATR}_{14}}, \quad -\text{DI}_{14} = 100 \cdot \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(-\text{DM})}{\text{ATR}_{14}}$$ $$\text{DX} = 100 \cdot \frac{\vert{}+\text{DI} - -\text{DI}\vert{}}{+\text{DI} + -\text{DI}}, \quad \text{ADX}_{14} = \text{EMA}_{14}(\text{DX})$$

5. Fixed Entry, Take-Profit Target, and Stop-Loss Levels

Establishes asymmetric Risk-to-Reward ratio ($k_{\text{TP}} : k_{\text{SL}} = 2.5 : 1$).

$$\text{Target}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(k_{\text{TP}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(k_{\text{SL}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$ $$\text{Target}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(k_{\text{TP}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(k_{\text{SL}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$

6. Adaptive Feedback Optimization Rules

Evaluates historical performance to self-adjust trend entry filters dynamically.

$$\text{WinRate} = \frac{N_{\text{TargetHit}}}{N_{\text{Closed}}}, \quad \text{ProfitFactor} = \frac{\sum \text{PnL}_{\text{Wins}}}{\sum \vert{}\text{PnL}_{\text{Losses}}\vert{}}$$ $$\theta_{\text{ADX}}^{(t+1)} = \begin{cases} \min(30.0, \, \theta_{\text{ADX}}^{(t)} + 1.0), & \text{if } \text{WinRate} < 0.40 \text{ or } \text{ProfitFactor} < 1.1 \\ \max(18.0, \, \theta_{\text{ADX}}^{(t)} - 0.5), & \text{if } \text{WinRate} \ge 0.55 \text{ and } \text{ProfitFactor} \ge 1.8 \end{cases}$$
