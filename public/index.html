⚙️ 1. Execution Architecture & System Workflow
Step 1: REST Ingestion & IST Timezone Normalization
Every 30 seconds, the backend queries Delta Exchange India REST endpoints (`/v2/history/candles`) to fetch 500 OHLCV candles (~5.2 days for 15m; ~20.8 days for 1h). Epoch UTC timestamps are converted to Indian Standard Time ($\text{IST} = \text{UTC} + 5:30$).
Step 2: Vectorized Indicator Array Matrix
Computes 9 technical indicators simultaneously using native `pandas` and `numpy`: EMAs (20, 50, 200), ATR (14), ATR Expansion Slope ($\Delta \text{ATR}$), Chaikin Money Flow ($\text{CMF}_{20}$), RSI (14), ADX (14), Volume SMA (20), Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std), and Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR).
Step 3: High-Probability Multi-Filter Gate Matrix
Applies a strict 5-part Boolean gate check across a 2-candle window: Squeeze release trigger, Volume surge ($\ge 1.05 \times \text{SMA}_{20}$), Chaikin Money Flow accumulation ($\text{CMF} \ge +0.03$), RSI Non-Exhaustion ($35 \le \text{RSI} \le 68$), and EMA trend alignment.
Step 4: Fixed-Target Position Locking State Machine
When an active trade triggers, entry price, stop-loss, and take-profit targets are locked in state memory. Levels remain frozen across API refreshes until intra-candle price action breaches TP or SL.
Step 5: Dynamic Breakeven Trailing & Journal Logging
Once floating profit touches $+1.0 \times \text{ATR}$, Stop Loss automatically moves to Breakeven ($\text{Entry} + 0.1 \times \text{ATR}$). When closed, the outcome is recorded into the Signal Journal.

🧮 5. Indicator Mathematical Formulas & Derivations

1. Exponential Moving Average ($\text{EMA}_N$)

Weighted moving average giving higher weight to recent price observations.

$$\alpha = \frac{2}{N + 1}, \quad \text{EMA}_N[t] = \left(C_t \cdot \alpha\right) + \left(\text{EMA}_N[t-1] \cdot (1 - \alpha)\right)$$

2. Average True Range ($\text{ATR}_{14}$)

Measures market volatility to dynamically scale stop-loss and take-profit targets.

$$\text{TR}_t = \max\left(H_t - L_t, \, \vert{}H_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}, \, \vert{}L_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}\right), \quad \text{ATR}_{14} = \frac{1}{14} \sum_{i=0}^{13} \text{TR}_{t-i}$$

3. TTM Volatility Squeeze Bands

Evaluates Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std) relative to Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR).

$$\text{BB}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \mu_{20} \pm 2.0 \cdot \sigma_{20}, \quad \text{KC}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \text{EMA}_{20} \pm 1.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_On} = (\text{BB}_{\text{Lower}} > \text{KC}_{\text{Lower}}) \land (\text{BB}_{\text{Upper}} < \text{KC}_{\text{Upper}})$$

4. Chaikin Money Flow ($\text{CMF}_{20}$)

Quantifies institutional buying/selling accumulation over a 20-period rolling window.

$$\text{MFM}_t = \frac{(C_t - L_t) - (H_t - C_t)}{H_t - L_t + \epsilon}, \quad \text{CMF}_{20} = \frac{\sum_{i=0}^{19} (\text{MFM}_{t-i} \cdot V_{t-i})}{\sum_{i=0}^{19} V_{t-i}}$$

5. Relative Strength Index ($\text{RSI}_{14}$)

Measures speed and velocity of price movements to prevent buying overbought momentum peaks.

$$\text{RS} = \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(\text{Gain})}{\text{EMA}_{14}(\text{Loss}) + \epsilon}, \quad \text{RSI}_{14} = 100 - \left( \frac{100}{1 + \text{RS}} \right)$$

6. Fixed Target & Dynamic Breakeven Stop Adjustment

Establishes asymmetric Risk-to-Reward ratio ($k_{\text{TP}} : k_{\text{SL}} = 2.5 : 1$) and locks risk-free status at $+1.0\text{x ATR}$.

$$\text{Target}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(k_{\text{TP}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Initial}} = \text{Entry} - \left(k_{\text{SL}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$ $$\text{SL}_{\text{Trailing}} = \begin{cases} \text{Entry} + \left(0.1 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), & \text{if } H_t \ge \text{Entry} + \left(1.0 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right) \text{ (Long)} \\ \text{Entry} - \left(0.1 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), & \text{if } L_t \le \text{Entry} - \left(1.0 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right) \text{ (Short)} \end{cases}$$
