⚙️ System Execution Architecture & Pipeline
Step 1: REST Ingestion & IST Timezone Normalization
Queries Delta Exchange India REST API endpoints (`/v2/history/candles`) every 30 seconds to fetch 500 OHLCV candles (~5.2 days for 15m; ~20.8 days for 1h). Epoch UTC timestamps are converted to IST (UTC + 5:30).
Step 2: Vectorized Analytics & Indicator Matrix
Computes technical indicators using native `pandas` and `numpy`: EMA (20, 50, 200), ATR (14), Bollinger Bands (20, 2.0 std), Keltner Channels (20, 1.5 ATR), TTM Squeeze Duration, ADX (14), and Volume SMA (20).
Step 3: Signal Classification & Multi-Filter Engine
Evaluates breakout triggers across a 2-candle window: Squeeze release trigger, Volume surge ($\ge 1.05 \times \text{SMA}_{20}$ for 15m; $\ge 1.08 \times \text{SMA}_{20}$ for 1h), Trend alignment ($\text{Price} > \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Long, $\text{Price} < \text{EMA}_{200}$ for Short), and Momentum positivity ($\text{Squeeze\_Mom} > 0$).
Step 4: Fixed-Target Position Locking & State Machine
Upon trade trigger, entry price, stop-loss, and take-profit targets are locked in state memory. Levels remain frozen across API refreshes until intra-candle price action hits TP or SL.
Step 5: Deterministic History Scanner & Journal Logging
Processes all 500 bars sequentially to detect historical trade entries/exits, ensuring that serverless container restarts do not lose signal history or active position state.

🧮 Mathematical Formulas & Quantitative Derivations

1. TTM Volatility Squeeze Condition

Detects volatility compression when Bollinger Bands fall inside Keltner Channels.

$$\text{BB}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \mu_{20} \pm 2.0 \cdot \sigma_{20}$$ $$\text{KC}_{\text{Upper/Lower}} = \text{EMA}_{20} \pm 1.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_On} = (\text{BB}_{\text{Lower}} > \text{KC}_{\text{Lower}}) \land (\text{BB}_{\text{Upper}} < \text{KC}_{\text{Upper}})$$

2. Average True Range ($\text{ATR}_{14}$)

Measures market volatility to dynamically scale stop-loss and take-profit targets.

$$\text{TR}_t = \max\left(H_t - L_t, \, \vert{}H_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}, \, \vert{}L_t - C_{t-1}\vert{}\right)$$ $$\text{ATR}_{14} = \frac{1}{14} \sum_{i=0}^{13} \text{TR}_{t-i}$$

3. TTM Momentum Histogram

Calculates linear regression delta against the midpoint of price extremes and EMA 20.

$$\Delta_{\text{Mid}} = \frac{\max(H_{20}) + \min(L_{20})}{2}$$ $$\text{Squeeze\_Mom} = C_t - \left( \frac{\Delta_{\text{Mid}} + \text{EMA}_{20}}{2} \right)$$

4. Average Directional Index ($\text{ADX}_{14}$)

Filters out non-trending market chop by requiring trend intensity to breach timeframe-calibrated threshold $\theta_{\text{ADX}}$.

$$+\text{DM} = H_t - H_{t-1}, \quad -\text{DM} = L_{t-1} - L_t$$ $$+\text{DI}_{14} = 100 \cdot \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(+\text{DM})}{\text{ATR}_{14}}, \quad -\text{DI}_{14} = 100 \cdot \frac{\text{EMA}_{14}(-\text{DM})}{\text{ATR}_{14}}$$ $$\text{DX} = 100 \cdot \frac{\vert{}+\text{DI} - -\text{DI}\vert{}}{+\text{DI} + -\text{DI}}, \quad \text{ADX}_{14} = \text{EMA}_{14}(\text{DX})$$

5. Fixed Entry, Take-Profit Target, and Stop-Loss Levels

Establishes asymmetric Risk-to-Reward ratio ($k_{\text{TP}} : k_{\text{SL}} = 2.5 : 1$ for 15M; $2.8 : 1$ for 1H).

$$\text{Target}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(k_{\text{TP}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bullish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(k_{\text{SL}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$ $$\text{Target}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} - \left(k_{\text{TP}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right), \quad \text{SL}_{\text{Bearish}} = \text{Entry} + \left(k_{\text{SL}} \cdot \text{ATR}_{14}\right)$$

6. Volume Expansion Filter Condition

Ensures institutional volume confirms the squeeze release.

$$\text{Volume\_SMA}_{20} = \frac{1}{20} \sum_{i=0}^{19} V_{t-i}$$ $$\text{Volume\_Surge} = V_t \ge \alpha_{\text{vol}} \cdot \text{Volume\_SMA}_{20} \quad (\alpha_{\text{vol}} = 1.05 \text{ for 15M}, 1.08 \text{ for 1H})$$
